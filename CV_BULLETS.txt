FoodRescue | Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, JWT
• Built a RESTful backend for redistributing surplus food from providers to NGOs/volunteers, covering donation listing, claiming, pickup assignment and expiry tracking.
• Designed a normalized PostgreSQL schema with relational constraints and indexes, and implemented JWT authentication with role-based authorization.
• Implemented transaction-based donation claiming with row-level locking to prevent race conditions and inconsistent inventory during concurrent claims.
